    What this model does and does not include

    Motion follows m d²r/dt² = q E(r, t) in three dimensions. Einzel lenses and aperture plates are axisymmetric, so their fields are solved on a two-dimensional (z, r) grid and rotation supplies the third dimension exactly. A quadrupole is not axisymmetric, so it is solved on a transverse (x, y) grid instead.

    Each element is solved in isolation, with grounded end faces, so its fringe field stops at its own boundary. Leave a drift between active elements; the readout warns when you do not. Quadrupole rods are modelled with a hard edge, so entrance and exit fringe fields — a real cause of transmission loss in a mass filter — are absent.

    Also absent: magnetic forces, buffer-gas collisions, image charges and relativistic correction. Each omission and its consequences are listed in docs/PHYSICS.md.
